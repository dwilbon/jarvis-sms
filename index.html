Jarvis SMS program – Damion Wilbon

Operated by Damion Wilbon, Michigan · +1 (618) 543-0076

Jarvis is Damion Wilbon's automated AI scheduling assistant. It reads Damion's calendar and, for people who have opted in, sends a text the day before a plan with Damion to confirm it's still on, and replies to their texts about scheduling or questions for Damion. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.
