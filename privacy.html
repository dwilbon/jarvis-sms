Privacy Policy

Damion Wilbon – Jarvis SMS program
Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Damion Wilbon (“Damion,” “we,” “us”), an individual in Michigan, collects and uses information through the Jarvis text messaging program. Jarvis is Damion's automated AI scheduling assistant. Messages are sent from +1 (618) 543-0076. This policy applies only to the Jarvis SMS program.

No sharing of mobile information

We do not share, sell, rent, or provide your mobile phone number, text messaging opt-in data, or messaging consent to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories of information described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile phone number and, if you provide it, your first name
  • A record of your opt-in (the keyword you texted, such as JARVIS or START, and the date and time) and any opt-out requests
  • The content of text messages you exchange with Jarvis
  • Plan details on Damion's calendar that involve you (such as the date, time, and description of the plan)

How we use information

We use this information only to operate the Jarvis SMS program: to send you a confirmation text the day before plans you have with Damion, to read and respond to your replies about scheduling or questions for Damion, to honor HELP and STOP requests, and to keep a record of your consent. We do not use it for marketing or advertising.

Service providers

To deliver the program, information is processed by service providers acting on our behalf: our SMS provider (Telnyx) and wireless carriers to send and receive messages, and an AI service provider that helps Jarvis draft replies. These providers may use the information only to provide their service to us, not for their own marketing. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies (typically 1–4 messages per month, plus replies to messages you send). Message and data rates may apply.

How to opt in, opt out, and get help

You opt in by following the steps on our opt-in page and texting JARVIS to +1 (618) 543-0076. Reply STOP at any time to opt out; you will receive one confirmation message and no further messages. Reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP for help, or email damionwilbon@gmail.com.

Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above (opt-in and opt-out records are kept as long as required to honor your choices) and take reasonable steps to protect it. You may email us to ask that we delete your information.

Children

The Jarvis SMS program is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last updated.
